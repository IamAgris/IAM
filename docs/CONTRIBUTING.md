# Contributing to IAM

Thank you for your interest in contributing to IAM. IAM is source-available under the [IAM Controlled Community Licence](../LICENSES/IAM-COMMUNITY-LICENSE.md). Community contributions are welcome for noncommercial use and development.

## How to Contribute

1. **Read the licence** – Review the [IAM Controlled Community Licence](../LICENSES/IAM-COMMUNITY-LICENSE.md) to understand what is permitted.
2. **Sign the CLA** – Before your Contribution can be merged, you must sign the [Contributor Licence Agreement (CLA.md)](CLA.md). The CLA lets you retain copyright while granting IAM the rights it needs to use and commercially license your Contribution.
3. **Open a pull request** – Submit your changes via a pull request to the official IAM GitHub repository.
4. **Identify third-party code** – If your Contribution includes third-party code, disclose it and confirm its licence is compatible.

## What You Can Do

- Read and study the source code;
- Personally experiment for noncommercial purposes;
- Conduct academic and educational research;
- Perform noncommercial testing;
- Submit bug reports;
- Propose improvements;
- Submit pull requests (subject to the CLA);
- Develop approved noncommercial extensions;
- Participate in approved research projects.

## What Requires Separate Authorisation

Commercial Use of IAM is not permitted under the Community Licence. This includes selling IAM, commercially hosting it, offering it as a paid SaaS product, or incorporating it into commercial products. Contact the Licensor for commercial licensing terms.

## Code of Conduct

Please be respectful and constructive in discussions, issues, and pull requests.
