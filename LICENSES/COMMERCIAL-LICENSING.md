# IAM Commercial Licensing

Commercial Use of IAM is not permitted under the IAM Controlled Community Licence. If you wish to engage in any Commercial Use, you must obtain a separate commercial licence from the Licensor.

## What Requires a Commercial Licence

The following activities require separate authorisation and a commercial licence:

- Selling IAM or any Derivative Work;
- Commercially hosting IAM for customers or third parties;
- Offering IAM as a paid software-as-a-service (SaaS) product;
- Incorporating IAM into a commercial product or service;
- Commercial redistribution of IAM or any Derivative Work;
- Selling modified versions of IAM;
- Commercially licensing IAM's proprietary components to third parties;
- Using IAM's name, logo, trademarks, or branding to imply official endorsement.

## How to Request a Commercial Licence

Contact the Licensor to negotiate commercial terms. Commercial licences are granted at the Licensor's sole discretion and may include:

- Scope of permitted Commercial Use;
- Territory and term;
- Royalty or fee structure;
- Attribution and branding requirements;
- Support and maintenance terms;
- Termination conditions.
